Private because there is nowhere to send it.

    

      

        
Check it yourself

        

          Open your browser's Network tab and paste something secret. No
          request goes out: the parsing happens in JSON.parse and
          DOMParser, right on the page.
        

      

      

        
Works offline after the first visit

        

          Open lint.one once and every tool is saved in your browser. After
          that it opens with no connection at all: on a plane, behind a
          firewall, or with the Wi-Fi off.
        

      

      

        
Made for large files

        

          Trees draw in chunks and searches stop at a sensible limit, so a
          40 MB file opens instead of freezing the tab.
